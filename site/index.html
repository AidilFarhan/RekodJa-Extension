Chrome extension

Save a job posting straight into your own Google Sheet.

RekodJa: Job Tracker helps you capture an application while the job page is open, so your job search stays organised.

Get the extension

01

Open a job posting

Click the extension while viewing a job page. It reads the page title and relevant job metadata to suggest the company and role.

02

Choose your sheet

Connect your Google account and select the spreadsheet tab where you track your applications.

03

Save and continue

Add your application date and save. The job is added to your chosen Google Sheet with status and source fields.

Your data, your control

Built to work directly with your Google account.

The extension does not operate a developer server. Job details are sent directly from your browser to the Google Sheet you select, using your Google account permission. Optional Gmail scanning is only started when you choose it.
